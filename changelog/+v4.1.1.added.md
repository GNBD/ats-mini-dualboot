The boot manager shows a **first-run coach-mark guide**: on the first boot it
dims the real main menu, outlines each tile in turn and shows a short callout
(the app slots, Firmware Update, Erase, Settings, the controls, how to open the
menu, and the web address). Rotate or hold to move on, click to skip; the guide
is remembered afterwards and can be replayed from `Settings > Guide`.
