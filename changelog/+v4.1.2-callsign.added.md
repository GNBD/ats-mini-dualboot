A **`Callsign`** tile was added to `Settings`, setting the **boot splash**
heading and the recovery menu header. The big `Boot Manager` splash line — and
the recovery main-menu title — become your callsign (printable ASCII, up to 16
characters); an optional robot-style **Greeting** line appears under the splash
title (e.g. `WELCOME`), and the bottom splash version line becomes
`Boot Manager v<version> (DES)`. A blank field restores the default text and the
greeting line is hidden when the greeting is off. The value and greeting can be
edited either on the device (on-screen keypad, like the Wi-Fi password) or from
the web **Status** card. The extra `Settings` tile is laid out in a 4x2 grid of
smaller tiles (one cell empty), so `Settings` stays on a single page.
